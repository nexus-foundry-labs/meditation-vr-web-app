# Design Lead Visual Direction — Meditation VR Web App

## Project
Browser-based VR-style waiting-room demo for a calming therapeutic meditation experience focused on:

- Creative clarity
- Calm
- Emotional safety
- Gentle guided visualization
- Vision-board style reflection

## Design Intent

The first demo should feel like entering a soft, protected, light-filled space where the user can breathe, imagine, and orient toward possibility without pressure. The visual language should avoid clinical, sterile, or overly mystical cues. The tone should be therapeutic but approachable: warm, grounded, spacious, and quietly uplifting.

The experience should support the emotional sequence:

1. Safe arrival
2. Calming reset
3. Creative clarity visualization
4. Affirmation close

## Core Emotional Qualities

### Calm Safety
Use soft gradients, low-contrast forms, rounded geometry, slow motion, and familiar natural cues. The user should feel that nothing will surprise, judge, or rush them.

### Creative Clarity
Introduce luminous spatial focus points, gentle floating cards, translucent boards, and light-path motifs. The experience should suggest ideas becoming clearer, not “productivity” or performance.

### Hopeful Grounding
Use warm light, dawn-like color transitions, and stable horizon lines. Avoid chaotic particle fields, harsh neon, dark voids, or overstimulating effects.
