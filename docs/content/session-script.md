# First-Session Script — Creative Clarity, Calm, and Safety

## Purpose
This script defines the first guided session for the browser VR prototype. It should be implemented as short, sequential prompts that users can preview, advance through, or revisit without leaving the app.

## Session Length
Recommended prototype length: 3–5 minutes.

## Session Structure

1. Safe Arrival
2. Calming Reset
3. Creative Clarity Visualization
4. Vision-Board Reflection
5. Affirmation Close

## Script Data Requirements

The app should include structured session content, such as JSON or JavaScript data, containing:

- Step ID
- Step title
- Step category
- Prompt text
- Affirmation text
- Visual direction
- Suggested ambient scene state

Recommended minimum for first demo:
- 8 guided steps
- 6 vision-board themes
- 6 affirmations

## Guided Session Steps

### Step 1: Safe Arrival

**Title:** Arrive Gently  
**Category:** Safe Arrival  
**Prompt:**  
You are invited to settle into this quiet space. There is nothing to solve right now.

**Affirmation:**  
I am allowed to arrive exactly as I am.

**Visual Direction:**  
Soft ambient light, slow-moving particles, spacious horizon, low contrast.

---

### Step 2: Notice Support

**Title:** Notice What Holds You  
**Category:** Safe Arrival  
**Prompt:**  
Let your attention rest on the surface beneath you. Notice that you are supported in this moment.

**Affirmation:**  
I can let support meet me here.

**Visual Direction:**  
Gentle floor glow, warm neutral tones, grounded visual plane.

---

### Step 3: Soften the Breath

**Title:** Make Space to Breathe  
**Category:** Calming Reset  
**Prompt:**  
Take a slow breath in, then let it leave without effort. If your mind wanders, you can return to the next breath.

**Affirmation:**  
My breath can be simple and easy.

**Visual Direction:**  
Subtle pulsing light synchronized with a slow breathing rhythm.

---

### Step 4: Release Pressure

**Title:** Nothing to Force  
**Category:** Calming Reset  
**Prompt:**  
There is no need to create a perfect feeling. Let the moment be soft enough for whatever is here.

**Affirmation:**  
I can be gentle with myself.

**Visual Direction:**  
Diffuse glow, slow drift, softening edges.

---

### Step 5: Open the Inner Canvas

**Title:** A Clear Inner Canvas  
**Category:** Creative Clarity  
**Prompt:**  
Imagine a calm, open space where a new idea can safely appear. Notice its colors, shapes, or atmosphere.

**Affirmation:**  
Clarity can arrive softly.

**Visual Direction:**  
Spacious sky-like environment, pale blues, lavender, warm white light.

---

### Step 6: Choose a Guiding Image

**Title:** Let One Image Come Forward  
**Category:** Creative Clarity  
**Prompt:**  
Allow one image, symbol, or word to become a gentle guide. It does not need to make sense yet.

**Affirmation:**  
I can trust the image that supports me.

**Visual Direction:**  
Floating cards or luminous panels with calm symbolic visuals.

---

### Step 7: Build the Vision

**Title:** Shape the Feeling  
**Category:** Vision Board  
**Prompt:**  
Choose the visual theme that feels most supportive. Let it become a quiet reminder of where you are growing.

**Affirmation:**  
My future can be shaped with care.

**Visual Direction:**  
Vision-board area with selectable prompt cards and generated-image style preview panels.

---

### Step 8: Carry It Forward

**Title:** Take One Thing With You  
**Category:** Affirmation Close  
**Prompt:**  
Choose one word, image, or feeling to carry into the rest of your day.

**Affirmation:**  
I leave with calm, safety, and creative clarity.

**Visual Direction:**  
Warm closing light, gentle fade, stable horizon.

## Standalone Affirmation Bank

These affirmations may be used during the session, on cards, or in the closing screen.

1. I am safe enough to soften.
2. I can move at the pace of calm.
3. My ideas can arrive without pressure.
4. I can choose what supports me.
5. Clarity can be gentle.
6. I am allowed to begin again.
7. My imagination can be a safe place.
8. I can carry one steady image with me.
9. I do not have to force the next step.
10. I can meet this moment with care.

## Content Safety Notes

- Do not ask users to revisit painful memories.
- Do not frame the experience as treatment.
- Do not promise relief, healing, or clinical outcomes.
- Do not use manipulative urgency.
- Do not include religious assumptions.
- Always preserve user choice.
